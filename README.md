**NAME**
<span title="Aung Lwin Oo">ALO</span>, known to some as Gene: software team lead, builder of developer tools, weekend teacher

**DESCRIPTION**
I build software, improve how teams develop and operate it, and teach what I learn.

For more than ten years I have worked at 365 Retail Markets across backend services,
point-of-sale software and cloud infrastructure: AWS with Terraform, release automation,
and lately AI tooling (MCP servers, agent skills, repeatable dev environments) that helps
developers, QA and support find answers faster.

Outside work I volunteer as tech lead and secretary for Win Metta, a California nonprofit,
and teach a few middle and high school students about coding, security, the internet and AI
on weekends, for free.
